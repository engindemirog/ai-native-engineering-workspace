# Prompt: Intent & Clarify

```
Role: Analyst (docs/roles/analyst.md). We will work on: "<one-sentence feature description>".
There is NO spec yet; we clarify intent first. Produce:

1) INTENT draft (3–5 sentences, BUSINESS language): who wants this and why; what success looks
   like; what is deliberately NOT being done.
2) CLARIFY questions: only questions whose answers are REQUIRED before a spec can be written
   (boundaries, empty/error cases, access, volumes, performance expectations). For each question
   add your own recommendation and rationale — I decide.

Do NOT write code, solutions, or endpoints. Intent and questions; nothing else.
```
